Include separate sections for recorded user/product signal, corpus and provenance, competitor baseline, measured results, gaps/limits, open questions, a five-item owner evidence request, and a specific continue/pause/pivot/stop decision.
2. Create `reports/M0/evidence.json` with schema version, counts, structured measured results, availability/gap reasons, and source records `{path, sha256, role}`.
