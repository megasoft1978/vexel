8. Summary and future work
Cell streams are constructed by spatially specializing a vector
graphics description to the cells of a lattice using a fast algorithm.
These streams allow efficient random-access evaluation of com-
posited layers of filled and stroked shapes, complete with
transparency, color gradients, and high-quality antialiasing.
