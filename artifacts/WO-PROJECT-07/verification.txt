Implemented periodic background recovery capture for dirty native projects. The scene is captured through the immutable document-edit snapshot API; timeline source bytes are pinned independently of history eviction; asset descriptors are copied and payload slices remain borrowed from the stable loaded project. A 30-second monotonic interval is used. The worker materializes and publishes an atomic capsule without mutating dirty or saved-state markers. The source file stamp is verified before and after publication; a capsule is removed if the stamp changes during writing. A concise editor status reports success/failure. Recovery preparation failures report a nonmodal status.
