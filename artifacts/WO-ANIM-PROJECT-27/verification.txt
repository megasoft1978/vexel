## Scope and remaining limitations

No comprehensive UI/E2E, corpus, fuzz, performance, or release suite was run.
The focused tests prove the native bootstrap and save-conversion slice, not the
overall product goals. Key actions still require an identified SVG element;
automatic stable-ID assignment remains follow-up work. The app's standard
Undo/Redo commands are not yet integrated with native timeline session history.
Full Inkscape SVG coverage, large-document performance targets, broad animation
authoring, MCP long-operation progress/cancellation and live-client evidence,
and final acceptance remain open.
